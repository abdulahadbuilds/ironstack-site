Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

IronStack Studio ("we", "us") builds websites and digital content for small businesses. This policy explains what information we collect through ironstackstudio.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

We only collect information you choose to send us:

  • When you fill in our mockup request form: your name, business name, email address, current website (if you add it) and your message.
  • When you email us: your email address and anything you include in your message.

We do not ask for payment details on this website and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request and prepare your free homepage mockup.
  • To discuss, quote and deliver a project you ask us for.
  • To keep basic records of our conversations with you.

We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your permission.

Services we use

To run this website and our communication, we rely on a few trusted providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Netlify hosts this website and receives form submissions.
  • Spaceship handles our domain and forwards emails sent to our address.
  • Google (Gmail) is where we receive and reply to email.
  • Google Fonts and public code libraries (cdnjs, unpkg) deliver fonts and scripts, which means your browser connects to them and they may see your IP address.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security and performance.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages for as long as needed to respond and, if we work together, for the length of the project and a reasonable period after it for our records. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email hello@ironstackstudio.com and we will reply within a reasonable time. If we contact you by phone and you ask us not to call again, we will remove your number right away.

Changes

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.
